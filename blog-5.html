The Ultimate Guide to Maintaining UPVC Windows

Well Maintained Today, Worry Free Tomorrow

UPVC windows are famously low-maintenance compared to wood or traditional aluminium. However, a little care goes a long way in keeping them looking brand new and functioning perfectly for decades. Here is your complete maintenance checklist:

1. Clean Frames Regularly

Wipe down your UPVC frames with a soft cloth and warm soapy water at least twice a year. This removes dust, grime, and pollutants that can dull the finish over time. A gentle non-abrasive cleaner works wonders for stubborn marks.

2. Keep Tracks Clean

Remove dirt and debris from window and door tracks to ensure smooth sliding. A vacuum with a narrow nozzle attachment works great for getting into tight spaces. Clean tracks prevent drainage blockages and ensure smooth operation.

3. Check Hardware & Hinges

Inspect hinges and locks regularly and tighten if needed. Look for any signs of wear or looseness in handles and locking mechanisms. Early detection prevents bigger issues down the line.

4. Lubricate Moving Parts

Apply a silicone-based lubricant to hinges, locks, and sliding mechanisms once a year. This ensures smooth functionality and prevents squeaking. Avoid oil-based lubricants as they can attract dust.

5. Avoid Harsh Chemicals

Never use abrasive or chemical cleaners on UPVC surfaces. Products containing acetone, bleach, or scouring agents can damage the surface finish. Stick to mild soap and water for the best results.
