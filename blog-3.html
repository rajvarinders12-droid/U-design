Top Window Design Trends for Modern Architecture

Style, Function, Innovation � All Through Your Windows

Windows are more than just openings � they define light, space, efficiency, and the character of modern architecture. Here are the top design trends shaping homes and commercial spaces today:

1. Floor-to-Ceiling Windows

Maximize natural light and create a seamless indoor-outdoor connection. These dramatic expanses of glass are made possible by strong aluminium frames that can support larger panes without compromising structural integrity.

2. Minimalist Frames

Slim profiles and clean lines for a sleek, contemporary look. Modern aluminium profiles can be as thin as 20mm while still providing excellent structural support and weather resistance.

3. Corner Windows

Open up views and bring in more light from multiple angles. Corner windows eliminate the traditional pillar, creating a stunning architectural feature that makes rooms feel more spacious and connected to the outdoors.

4. Dark Frame Finishes

Matte black and anthracite grey finishes have overtaken traditional white frames, giving homes a bold, industrial-chic edge. These finishes are available in both UPVC and aluminium.

5. Slide & Fold Systems

Bi-fold doors that open an entire wall to the outdoors are incredibly popular for living rooms, patios, and balconies. They fold neatly to one side, creating unobstructed views and seamless transitions.
